## What to notice

- **Shell/chrome** — the browser-based OS surface, not a raw website.
- **Packs/apps** — installable KHAL units with `khal-app.json` and runtime proof.
- **Terminal pack** — where local commands and dry-runs are visible.
- **Settings/source panels** — where identity, auth, and app metadata become visible.

## Visual proof rule

A screenshot proves only what is visible. Pair it with command/API proof when claiming an install, auth, release, or target state.
