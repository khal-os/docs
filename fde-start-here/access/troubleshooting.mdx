Quick answers for the handful of things that trip up most new FDEs during [Access](/fde-start-here/access/overview). For anything else, see the general [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting) page.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My invite code says it's already used" icon="ticket">
    Invite codes are single-use by design — the first successful `khal fde enroll` consumes it permanently. If you're seeing this and you're sure this is your first attempt, you may have already enrolled — check with `khal fde status` before assuming otherwise. If you really haven't enrolled yet, ask your admin to mint a fresh one; there's no way to "unconsume" the old one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="`khal fde ssh` says I'm not approved yet" icon="lock">
    This is expected, not an error. Every workstation's SSH lease is gated by the Deploy Ledger until a lead or admin approves it — the workstation existing and the workstation being reachable are two different steps. Ping whoever is onboarding you and point them at [Mint an invite and approve access](/fde-start-here/access/admin-guide). While you wait, `khal fde status` and `khal fde ssh --dry-run` both work and mutate nothing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which --target do I use?" icon="bullseye">
    `--target` is an environment label, not a URL — `dev`, `hml`, or `prod`. Almost every new FDE should use `--target dev` when they enroll. `hml`/`prod` targets belong to specific customer engagements and come from your onboarding lead, not a guess. See the [environment ladder](/fde-start-here/environments) for what each rung means.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Minting an invite fails or says access denied" icon="ban">
    `khal fde invite create` is superadmin-only, and only accepts `@namastex.ai` addresses. If you're not a superadmin, you can't mint one — ask a superadmin to do it for you. If the email isn't `@namastex.ai`, that's also a hard no; there's no workaround.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I don't know where I am in the process" icon="map">
    ```bash
    khal fde status
    ```
    Run it any time, from mint through connect — it shows your current state and never mutates anything.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
