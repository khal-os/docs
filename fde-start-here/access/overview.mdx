When someone says an FDE has "access," they don't mean a dashboard account. They mean you can `ssh` into your own real, full-sudo Ubuntu workstation, running on KHAL's own infrastructure. Everything on this page is the path that gets you there.

<Note>
  Every command on these pages is the `khal fde` CLI (part of `app-kit`), talking to the platform API at `platform.khal.ai`. That host never changes. `--target` is an **environment label** — `dev`, `hml`, or `prod` — never a URL or hostname.
</Note>

## Two people, one flow

Getting access takes two roles working together:

- **An admin (or superadmin)** mints your invite and, later, approves your workstation's SSH lease.
- **You, the engineer** redeem the invite, request the workstation, and connect.

Nobody skips a step. You can't request a workstation without an invite, and you can't SSH in without an approval — even after the workstation itself exists.

## The flow, at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admin mints an invite">
    A superadmin creates your single-use invite code — the credential that bootstraps your identity. Nothing to install yet; this happens before anyone touches a CLI on your side.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You enroll">
    You redeem the invite and hand over your SSH public key with `khal fde enroll`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You request a workstation">
    `khal fde instance` provisions a real VM — cloned, booted, and wired into Tailscale automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admin approves SSH access">
    Your workstation exists, but its SSH lease stays gated until a lead or admin approves it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You connect">
    `khal fde ssh` drops you onto your own workstation. `khal fde status` tells you exactly where you are in this list at any point.
  </Step>
</Steps>
